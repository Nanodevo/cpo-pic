Glossary

Every abbreviation and named quantity used anywhere in this study, expanded and explained in one line. If a page uses a short form without expanding it, that is a bug in the page and this is the fallback.

Light in a waveguide

NANumerical aperture. √(n₁²−n₂²) for a fiber: the index step written as an angle.
VNormalized frequency, or V number. 2πa·NA/λ. Below 2.405 a step-index guide carries one mode.
bNormalized propagation constant. 0 at cutoff, 1 for a tightly held mode. Says where neff sits between cladding and core.
MFD, MFRMode field diameter / radius. Where the field amplitude falls to 1/e, equivalently intensity to 1/e².
neffEffective index. β/k₀. The index the mode behaves as though it is travelling in.
LPLinearly polarized modes. The simplified mode set valid only when the index contrast is small.
TE, TMTransverse electric / transverse magnetic. The two polarizations of a planar or rectangular guide.
TIRTotal internal reflection.
PDLPolarization dependent loss.
PSRPolarization splitter-rotator. Separates TE and TM and turns one into the other.
FSRFree spectral range. Spacing between a resonator's successive resonances.

Geometrical optics

LSALongitudinal spherical aberration. Distance along the axis between where edge rays and axial rays cross.
EFLEffective focal length. Measured from the principal plane, which sits inside the glass.
BFD, FFDBack / front focal distance. Measured from the last / first glass surface, which is where you can put a ruler.
MTFModulation transfer function. How much contrast an optic preserves against spatial frequency.
f/#f-number. Focal length divided by aperture diameter. The single number that says how hard a lens is.

Mathematics and methods

WKBWentzel-Kramers-Brillouin approximation. Solves d²ψ/dx² + Q(x)ψ = 0 when Q varies slowly, by integrating the local wavenumber. In a forbidden region it gives tunnelling as exp(−2∫κdx). In optics the same approximation is called the eikonal and is what produces rays.
∇²Laplacian. Divergence of the gradient, not divergence squared. Measures how far a point is from the average of its neighbours.
Airy functionExact solution of a linear potential, and therefore the universal solution near any simple turning point.
FDTDFinite-difference time-domain. Full-vector, handles sub-wavelength features and back-reflection. Expensive.
FEMFinite element method. Used here for mode solving on a cross-section.
BPMBeam propagation method. Fast for long, slowly varying structures. Fails at high contrast and cannot handle back-reflection.
EMEEigenmode expansion. The right tool for long adiabatic transitions such as tapers.
RCWARigorous coupled-wave analysis. For periodic structures: gratings, metasurfaces.
POPPhysical optics propagation. Zemax's mode for propagating a real beam rather than rays.

Materials and platforms

IOXIon exchange. Making a waveguide by swapping alkali ions in glass, with no deposition and no etching.
SOISilicon on insulator. The standard silicon photonics wafer: device silicon over buried oxide.
BOXBuried oxide. The insulator layer under the device silicon.
SiNSilicon nitride. Lower contrast than silicon, far lower loss, no two-photon absorption at telecom wavelengths.
InPIndium phosphide. Direct bandgap III-V: the platform that can make light, amplify it, and provide strong nonlinearity.
PCM, GSTPhase change material, typically germanium-antimony-tellurium. Non-volatile optical state.
SMFSingle-mode fiber. SMF-28 is Corning's standard telecom fiber.
CTECoefficient of thermal expansion. Mismatch between materials is a packaging failure mode.

Devices

PICPhotonic integrated circuit.
MZI, MZMMach-Zehnder interferometer / modulator. Split, phase-shift one arm, recombine.
EAMElectro-absorption modulator.
VπLVoltage-length product of a modulator. Lower is better; it trades against optical loss.
SOASemiconductor optical amplifier. A laser without a cavity. Its gain saturation is a usable nonlinearity.
XGM, XPMCross-gain / cross-phase modulation. One beam changing what another beam sees. The basis of all-optical logic.
DFB, DBRDistributed feedback / distributed Bragg reflector lasers. Single-wavelength sources.
PIN, APDp-i-n and avalanche photodiodes. APDs add internal gain and excess noise.
SPADSingle-photon avalanche diode. An APD biased above breakdown.
TIATransimpedance amplifier. Converts detector current to voltage; usually sets receiver noise.
AWGArrayed waveguide grating. An on-chip wavelength multiplexer.
MPOMulti-fiber push-on connector. MPO-16 carries sixteen fibers at 250 µm pitch.

Systems and links

CPOCo-packaged optics. Putting the photonics in the same package as the switch chip.
WDMWavelength division multiplexing. Many channels on one waveguide, one per wavelength.
BERBit error rate.
OSNROptical signal-to-noise ratio.
RINRelative intensity noise of a laser.
NEPNoise equivalent power. The optical power that gives a signal-to-noise ratio of one.
SMSRSide mode suppression ratio. How cleanly a laser runs on one mode.
ASEAmplified spontaneous emission. The noise an optical amplifier adds; what limits cascading gain stages.
O/E/OOptical-electrical-optical. Converting out of light and back, which costs energy and latency.

Nonlinear optics

χ(n)Susceptibility of order n. χ(1) is ordinary index and absorption, χ(2) needs a non-centrosymmetric crystal, χ(3) exists in everything.
TPATwo-photon absorption. Two photons together exceed the bandgap. The imaginary part of χ(3).
FCAFree carrier absorption. The carriers TPA creates then absorb more light. The practical power ceiling in silicon.
SPM, XPM, FWMSelf-phase, cross-phase modulation, four-wave mixing. Kerr effects.
Kerr effectIndex depending on intensity: n = n₀ + n₂I.

Measurement

OTDR, OFDROptical time / frequency domain reflectometry. Locate loss and reflections along a guide.
CutbackMeasure identical structures of different lengths; the slope is loss per unit length and the intercept is coupling.
VNAVector network analyser. Measures S-parameters; S21 gives electro-optic bandwidth.
PRBSPseudo-random binary sequence. The test pattern behind an eye diagram.
GRR, MSAGauge repeatability and reproducibility, measurement system analysis. Proving the instrument before trusting the difference.

Process and quality

DOEDesign of experiments. Vary factors deliberately rather than one at a time.
SPCStatistical process control.
Cp, CpkProcess capability indices. How well a distribution fits inside a specification.
FMEAFailure mode and effects analysis.
8DEight disciplines. A structured problem-solving report for an excursion.
DMAICDefine, measure, analyse, improve, control. The Six Sigma improvement cycle.
PDKProcess design kit. The foundry's rules and validated component library.
MPWMulti-project wafer. Sharing a mask set and a run between many customers.

Computing

OISCOne instruction set computer. Turing complete with a single instruction.
SUBLEQSubtract and branch if less than or equal to zero. The usual choice of that single instruction.
TOPSTera-operations per second.
2RReamplification and reshaping. Signal regeneration without retiming.
